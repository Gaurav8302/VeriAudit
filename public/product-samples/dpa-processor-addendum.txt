Data Processing Addendum (sample)

Section 1 Purpose
This addendum covers processor obligations for customer personal data.

Section 2 Required approval clause
Customer policy requires a written prior-approval clause before a processor may engage a sub-processor.
This addendum describes notification after the fact. It does not contain a written prior-approval clause.

Section 3 Security
The processor will apply reasonable technical measures. No independent audit right is stated.
