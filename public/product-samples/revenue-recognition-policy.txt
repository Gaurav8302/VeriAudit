Revenue Recognition Policy REV-POL-3 (sample)

Page 6
Section 4.1 Timing
Revenue is recognised when a performance obligation is satisfied and the customer has obtained control of the promised goods or services.

Page 7
Section 4.2 Recognition Criteria
Milestone M2 on logistics contracts is satisfied only after recorded delivery.
Do not recognise revenue for undelivered or partially delivered shipments.
Transaction REV-1042 class entries require a delivery confirmation before recognition.

Page 8
Section 4.3 Approval
The same person must not prepare and approve a material revenue entry.
Approvals above $250,000 require a second reviewer who is not the preparer.
